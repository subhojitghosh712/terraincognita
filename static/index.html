BEHIND THE EXPERIMENT

A window, not the whole picture.

We change two internal activations while the model runs. Its stored weights stay the same. Observing a change gives us evidence of influence; it does not establish two dedicated “evil neurons.”

What does the dose mean? +

The dose is a multiplier, not an absolute activation value or a percentage. A dose of zero leaves the neuron unchanged.

activation adjustment = dose × calibration scale

The published recipe scales each intervention using the typical residual-stream magnitude and the norm of the neuron's output-weight column. A dose of 1 is the published reference setting, not a proven safe maximum. Half the dose does not imply half the behavioral effect.

Calibration values are supplied by the local model server.

Both conditions receive the same prompt, brevity instruction, seed, and sampling settings. The original response may be reused when those inputs match; cached results are labeled.
